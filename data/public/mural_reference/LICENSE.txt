The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access photographs and object metadata
License: CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Official policy: https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access
Each object has isPublicDomain=true in its accompanying metadata/met-OBJECT_ID.json.
The manifest preserves original image URLs, object accession numbers, credit lines and SHA256 hashes.
The files are reference photographs of real mural objects, not expert disease annotations.
